About Pixel Forge

Pixel Forge is a powerful and intuitive pixel art generator that allows you to create stunning pixel art designs with ease. Whether you're a professional artist or just starting out, our tool provides everything you need to bring your creative visions to life.

Features

Customizable Grid

Create grids of any size up to 35x35 pixels

Color Picker

Choose from millions of colors for your artwork

Erase Tool

Easy erase functionality to correct mistakes

Touch Support

Works seamlessly on both desktop and mobile devices
